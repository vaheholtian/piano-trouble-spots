Timing tolerances & how it measures

Note length is key-down to key-up, compared with the reference MIDI’s note length. Sustain pedal resonance is not measured.

Your first note starts the clock. Later notes stay on the MIDI’s timeline; the app does not adjust the reference to follow your tempo.

Pitch identifies which key to time. Wrong, missing, or ambiguous notes are left unassessed. Fingering is not detected.

## Phase Goal

**As a** pianist drilling right-hand C D E F G alone at the FP-60X, **I want to** see wrong, missed and extra notes painted on the notation after each pass, with honest counts across the passes at one tempo, **so that** I can tell a recurring mistake from a one-off slip and trust what the score shows me.

<objective>
The tracer: one path, every layer. A marked pass of C D F F G on rung 1 goes raw events → PassSegmenter → `Align.alignPass` (fixed origin from the click timeline, slots, tokens, the in-time DP with the contract's cost model) → `Aggregate.foldSession` → `Paint.paintNotes` → a red E4 on the real notation and the D-19 sentence in the detail panel when it is clicked. Task 2 then makes that path trustworthy under real conditions the cross-AI review found: a pass is judged only once the click timeline covers it (live equals replay), a session is analysed and painted only against its own score across piece switches, deviations come from each matched event, and the detail panel regenerates. Every one of 03-01's twelve fixtures is green under node:test, and 03-01's headless harness turns fully green without being edited.

Purpose: ROADMAP success criteria 1, 2 (single-pass part) and 3 on rung 1, proven at the desk so the piano session (03-05) is spent on judgement, not defects.

Output: src/align.js, src/aggregate.js, src/paint.js, the capture-app hooks, the piece-rendered event, index.html analysis DOM, check-run-path list, test/align.test.cjs, test/aggregate.test.cjs.
</objective>

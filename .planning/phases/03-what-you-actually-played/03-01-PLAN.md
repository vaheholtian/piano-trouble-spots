4. **Slots, tokens and the cost model** (the algorithm contract, prose + one table): a *slot* is a distinct absolute onset (measure start plus note onset, compared with ScoreModel.compareRationals) holding every score note at that onset across both staves; slot order is the model's canonical order; a slot's expected time is the recorded click at origin index plus the onset in quarter beats, linearly interpolated between the two surrounding real clicks for a fractional onset (rung 5 has eighth notes); a slot is *reached* when its expected time ≤ pass end + grace, where pass end is the mark timestamp or, for a pass with no end timestamp, the last played note's timestamp. Played notes are grouped into *tokens*: a note-on within chordWindowMs of the token's first note-on joins it unless its pitch is already in the token (then it starts a new token). Alignment is a global dynamic-programming alignment over reached slots × tokens with three operations — pair a slot with a token, delete a slot (its notes missed), insert a token (its notes extras). Pair cost = per-notehead pitch cost + timing cost: inside a pair, equal pitches are played; the remaining slot pitches and token pitches are paired by nearest MIDI number as wrong notes at `substitution` each, leftover slot pitches are missed at `deletion` each, leftover token pitches are extras within the slot at `insertion` each; timing cost is `perBeat` × |token onset − expected| / local beat ms — linear, with no threshold, so "within roughly a slot's width" is a consequence, not a constant: a same-pitch note stays matched until its timing cost exceeds what an in-slot wrong note plus an extra would cost (about 0.9 of a beat under the initial weights). All costs are computed in integer thousandths of a weight unit (the timing term rounded once) so equal costs are exactly equal.

4. **Slots, tokens, readings and the cost model** (prose + one table). A *slot* is a distinct absolute onset (measure start plus onset via ScoreModel.compareRationals) holding every score note at that onset across both staves, in canonical order. Expected time = the recorded click at (origin index + reading lag + whole quarter beats), linearly interpolated to the next recorded click for a fractional onset; null when a needed click is absent. *Tokens*: note-ons in seq order; a note-on within chordWindowMs of the open token's first note-on joins it — a pitch already in the token joins as a duplicate. Global DP over reached slots × tokens: pair, delete a slot (notes missed), insert a token (notes extras). Pair cost = pitch cost + timing cost: equal pitches pair one-to-one as a multiset (played); remaining slot and token pitches are paired by the assignment with the minimum total semitone distance, each pair a wrong note at `substitution`; leftover slot pitches missed at `deletion` each; leftover token pitches (duplicates included) extras within the slot at `insertion` each; when more than one minimum-distance assignment labels different noteheads, those noteheads are unassessed ambiguous and their played pitches ambiguous played notes (the pair cost is the same either way). Timing cost = `perBeat` × |token first onset − expected| / local beat ms, linear, no threshold. All costs integer thousandths. deviationMs of a played note = its own matched event's timeStamp − the fixed-origin expected time (never the token's first onset, review 03-02 MEDIUM). **Readings (D-02 late entry):** lag bound H = the largest value, over the first `lateEntryProbeTokens` tokens, of (the index of the click nearest that token's onset among clicks at or after the origin, ties to the earlier click) minus the origin index, at least 0 — several tokens, so one stray opening note cannot pin H to 0 (review 03-03 HIGH round 2). Every lag h from 0 to H is a reading; a reading's pitch correspondence (timing cost) and gap keys use the click lag h later; its total cost is its DP cost plus `lateEntry` when h > 0. **Reach clock (the one switch for the pending user decision, review 03-01 HIGH round 2):** a slot is reached when its expected time is not null and is at or before end + markGraceBeats × localBeatMs(b), where b is the last click at or before the end (the origin index when none) and localBeatMs(i) is the gap from click i to the next recorded click (the previous gap when none); with `reachClock` `'origin'` (default, D-11 as written) the expected time used is the lag-0 time in every reading, so all readings share one reach set; with `'reading'` it is the reading's lagged time. The two agree at lag 0. No other rule reads this switch. The cheapest reading is chosen; entryLag = its h. deviationMs and the reported origin always use lag 0 — the origin never moves. **Ties:** two optimal alignments (within a reading or across equally cheap readings) that assign a slot or token differently make it ambiguous, extended over a run of consecutive identical-pitch-set slots (D-06); label-identical optimal readings report the smaller lag. **Restart rule:** a second run of tokens matching the pitch sets (unique pitches) of the first restartPrefixSlots slots, starting after the first run ends, with at least one inserted token → restarted. **Tempo rule:** clicks at or after the origin and strictly before the pass end must share the origin click's bpm, else tempo-changed. Precedence: tempo-changed > restarted > per-slot not-reached / ambiguous.

Only final passes are painted and counted; a pending pass is listed as finishing. Once final, appending clicks never changes `stableFields(result)` = the PassResult with `expectedTime` removed from every slot whose `reached` is false (review 03-02 HIGH round 2).

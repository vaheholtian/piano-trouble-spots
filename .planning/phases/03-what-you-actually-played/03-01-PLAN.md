4. **Slots, tokens, readings and the cost model** (prose + one table). A *slot* is a distinct absolute onset (measure start plus onset via ScoreModel.compareRationals) holding every score note at that onset across both staves, in canonical order. Expected time = the recorded click at (origin index + reading lag + whole quarter beats), linearly interpolated to the next recorded click for a fractional onset; null when a needed click is absent. *Tokens*: note-ons in seq order; a note-on within chordWindowMs of the open token's first note-on joins it — a pitch already in the token joins as a duplicate. Global DP over reached slots × tokens: pair, delete a slot (notes missed), insert a token (notes extras). Pair cost = pitch cost + timing cost: equal pitches pair one-to-one as a multiset (played); remaining slot and token pitches are paired by the assignment with the minimum total semitone distance, each pair a wrong note at `substitution`; leftover slot pitches missed at `deletion` each; leftover token pitches (duplicates included) extras within the slot at `insertion` each; when more than one minimum-distance assignment labels different noteheads, those noteheads are unassessed ambiguous and their played pitches ambiguous played notes (the pair cost is the same either way). Timing cost = `perBeat` × |token first onset − expected| / local beat ms, linear, no threshold. All costs integer thousandths. deviationMs of a played note = its own matched event's timeStamp − the fixed-origin expected time (never the token's first onset, review 03-02 MEDIUM). **Readings (D-02 late entry):** lag bound H = the index of the click nearest the first played note (ties to the earlier click) minus the origin index, at least 0. Every lag h from 0 to H is a reading; a reading's expected times, reach and gap keys use the click lag h later; its total cost is its DP cost plus `lateEntry` when h > 0. The cheapest reading is chosen; entryLag = its h. deviationMs and the reported origin always use lag 0 — the origin never moves. **Ties:** two optimal alignments (within a reading or across equally cheap readings) that assign a slot or token differently make it ambiguous, extended over a run of consecutive identical-pitch-set slots (D-06); label-identical optimal readings report the smaller lag. **Restart rule:** a second run of tokens matching the pitch sets (unique pitches) of the first restartPrefixSlots slots, starting after the first run ends, with at least one inserted token → restarted. **Tempo rule:** clicks at or after the origin and strictly before the pass end must share the origin click's bpm, else tempo-changed. Precedence: tempo-changed > restarted > per-slot not-reached / ambiguous.

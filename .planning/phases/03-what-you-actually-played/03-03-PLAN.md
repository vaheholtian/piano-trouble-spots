## Phase Goal

**As a** pianist drilling right-hand C D E F G alone at the FP-60X, **I want to** see wrong, missed and extra notes painted on the notation after each pass, with honest counts across the passes at one tempo, **so that** I can tell a recurring mistake from a one-off slip and trust what the score shows me.

<objective>
Complete every interpretation rule rung 1 needs before the user plays it at the piano (03-05): ambiguity instead of guessing (D-06, D-13), the length-independent late-entry reading the cross-AI review showed the in-time cost model cannot provide (D-02), and the whole-pass reasons — restart without a mark, a tempo change inside a pass with the corrected membership rule, and the no-origin edge (D-12, D-16). Each rule gets hand-derived fixtures first, then the code, then the tunables table if anything moved. Chords, both hands and the ladder regression wait until after the rung-1 piano gate (03-06), per the roadmap's rung order.

Purpose: ROADMAP success criterion 2 ("uncertain alignment ... visibly unassessed") and criterion 6's repeated pitches, ambiguous alignment, late entry, interrupted pass, restart and tempo-change cases, all on rung 1.

Output: thirteen new fixtures, ambiguity / lag readings / restart / tempo / no-origin handling in src/align.js, tests, and a tunables table that records any change.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

**As a** pianist drilling right-hand C D E F G alone at the FP-60X, **I want to** see wrong, missed and extra notes painted on the notation after each pass, with honest counts across the passes at one tempo, **so that** I can tell a recurring mistake from a one-off slip and trust what the score shows me.

<objective>
Expand the proven engine with the two rules that make the marks trustworthy on messy playing and on the chord rung: ambiguity detection (D-06, D-13 — say "I could not tell" instead of guessing) and per-notehead chord judgement across both staves (D-07, rungs 3 and 4). Each rule gets its hand-derived fixture first, then the code, then the doc's tunables table if anything moved.

Purpose: ROADMAP success criterion 2 ("without chords or small timing sloppiness producing false mistakes... uncertain alignment... visibly unassessed") and criterion 6's "repeated pitches" and "ambiguous alignment" cases.

Output: five new fixtures, ambiguity marking in src/align.js, tests, and a tunables table that records any change.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

**As a** pianist drilling right-hand C D E F G alone at the FP-60X, **I want to** see wrong, missed and extra notes painted on the notation after each pass, with honest counts across the passes at one tempo, **so that** I can tell a recurring mistake from a one-off slip and trust what the score shows me.

<objective>
Complete every interpretation rule rung 1 needs before the user plays it at the piano (03-05): ambiguity instead of guessing (D-06, D-13), the length-independent late-entry reading the cross-AI review showed the in-time cost model cannot provide (D-02), and the whole-pass reasons — restart without a mark, a tempo change inside a pass with the corrected membership rule, and the no-origin edge (D-12, D-16). Each rule gets hand-derived fixtures first, then the code, then the tunables table if anything moved. Left hand, both hands, chords and four bars wait until after the rung-1 piano gate and are then built and checked at the piano one rung at a time (03-06 rung 2, 03-07 rung 3, 03-08 rung 4, 03-09 rung 5), per the roadmap's rung order.

Purpose: ROADMAP success criterion 2 ("uncertain alignment ... visibly unassessed") and criterion 6's repeated pitches, ambiguous alignment, late entry, interrupted pass, restart and tempo-change cases, all on rung 1.

Round 2 (58daf59) adds: a lag bound over the first three played tokens, so a stray opening note cannot disable the late reading, with fixtures for an opening extra and a missing opening note; the pending reach decision pinned by two explicit-tunables fixtures behind the single `reachClock` switch; and a measured analysis budget (`scripts/check-align-perf.cjs`) for a realistic 50-pass session with a long wait before entry.

## Phase Goal

**As a** pianist drilling right-hand C D E F G alone at the FP-60X, **I want to** see wrong, missed and extra notes painted on the notation after each pass, with honest counts across the passes at one tempo, **so that** I can tell a recurring mistake from a one-off slip and trust what the score shows me.

<objective>
The rung-1 piano gate, ahead of every harder rung as the roadmap requires (review 03-06 HIGH). It re-runs every automated gate fresh and gives the user a plain rung-1 checklist whose every expected observation names the view to look at and the exact sentence. It then stops for the real checks at the Roland FP-60X:

- **Scenario A:** single passes covering every rung-1 rule, including the late-and-abandoned bar where the user decides the reach rule, a doubled E, and a BPM change inside a pass.
- **Scenario B:** twelve passes at one tempo showing the habit and the slip, then a separate, controlled BPM change right after a mark.

Rungs 2 to 5 (03-06 to 03-09) start only after approval. A false mark goes to gap closure first.

Round 2 (58daf59) fixes the checklist timing. A "ready rule" puts an explicit re-mark between reading the screen and the next attempt, so inspection never eats the next downbeat. Expectations no longer depend on hard-coded pass numbers. The tempo change right after a mark is its own step, scheduled so it can physically happen. Three probes are added: the late-and-abandoned reach decision, the doubled E (repeated pitch assigned by timing) and a within-pass tempo change.

This plan is a verification gate with no production code layer, so it leads with the automated gate rather than a tracer slice; the tracer for this phase is 03-02.

Purpose: ROADMAP Phase 3 success criteria 4 (rung 1), 5 and 6 (rules checked on relevant piano examples before the next rung) are the only proof that the marks say what actually happened on real hardware.

Output: README.md and fixtures/README.md updated, all gates green, and the user's two checkpoint responses recorded verbatim in the SUMMARY, including the reach choice.
</objective>

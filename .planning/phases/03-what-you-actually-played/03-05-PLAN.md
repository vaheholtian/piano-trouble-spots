## Phase Goal

**As a** pianist drilling right-hand C D E F G alone at the FP-60X, **I want to** see wrong, missed and extra notes painted on the notation after each pass, with honest counts across the passes at one tempo, **so that** I can tell a recurring mistake from a one-off slip and trust what the score shows me.

<objective>
The rung-1 piano gate, moved ahead of every harder rung as the roadmap requires (review 03-06 HIGH): re-run every automated gate fresh, give the user a plain rung-1 checklist whose every expected observation names the view to look at and the exact sentence, then stop for the real checks at the Roland FP-60X — Scenario A (single passes, every rung-1 rule) and Scenario B (twelve passes at one tempo: the habit and the slip, then a separate tempo switch). Chords, both hands and the ladder regression (03-06) start only after approval. A false mark goes to gap closure first.

This plan is a verification gate with no production code layer, so it leads with the automated gate rather than a tracer slice; the tracer for this phase is 03-02.

Purpose: ROADMAP Phase 3 success criteria 4 (rung 1) and 5 are the only proof that the marks say what actually happened on real hardware, and the ladder order is how the user builds trust one rung at a time.

Output: README.md and fixtures/README.md updated, all gates green, and the user's two checkpoint responses recorded verbatim in the SUMMARY.
</objective>

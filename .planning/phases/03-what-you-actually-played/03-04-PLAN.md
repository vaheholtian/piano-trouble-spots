## Phase Goal

**As a** pianist drilling right-hand C D E F G alone at the FP-60X, **I want to** see wrong, missed and extra notes painted on the notation after each pass, with honest counts across the passes at one tempo, **so that** I can tell a recurring mistake from a one-off slip and trust what the score shows me.

<objective>
Finish the minimal view the roadmap's 10+ pass experiment is read from: the single-pass view and the way back, the tempo-group selector, the extra-note glyphs at their gap positions with their counts and sentences, and the pass list as the navigation for all of it — then extend the headless harness to prove every piece in real Chrome. No engine changes; this plan runs in parallel with 03-03 and touches only DOM-side files.

Purpose: ROADMAP success criterion 5 (selecting a marked score note shows plain-language counts with assessed and unassessed totals) and criterion 3 (marks on the noteheads, extras included, not in a separate list).

Output: capture-app view switching and pass-list navigation, paint.js glyph positioning, index.html styling, seven-line check-paint harness.
</objective>

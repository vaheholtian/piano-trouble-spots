## Phase Goal

**As a** pianist drilling right-hand C D E F G alone at the FP-60X, **I want to** see wrong, missed and extra notes painted on the notation after each pass, with honest counts across the passes at one tempo, **so that** I can tell a recurring mistake from a one-off slip and trust what the score shows me.

<objective>
Finish the minimal view the roadmap's 10+ pass experiment is read from: the single-pass view and the way back, the tempo-group selector, the extra-note glyphs at their gap positions (including across a system break) with counts and sentences, the pass list as navigation with honest labels, and a detail panel that follows the view — then extend the headless harness to prove every piece in real Chrome, including the real-scheduler BPM-change case from the review. No engine changes; this plan runs in parallel with 03-03 and touches only DOM-side files, one pure geometry test and a Chrome timing script.

Purpose: ROADMAP success criterion 5 (selecting a marked score note shows plain-language counts with assessed and unassessed totals) and criterion 3 (marks on the noteheads, extras included, not in a separate list), ready for the rung-1 piano gate in 03-05.

Round 2 (58daf59): the Phase 2 capture regression is updated in the same task that introduces the new pass-list suffixes, so both browser harnesses agree; every sentence and count later in the paint scenario is reconciled with the extra passes this plan adds.

Round 3 (2098322) makes three changes. The pass view's gap sentence gets the whole PassResult, so ambiguity survives to the "at least" wording. The tempo selector is hidden in a pass view, and choosing a tempo always returns to that group's session view. A third task instruments the live callbacks and adds `scripts/check-live-perf.cjs`, which measures the complete finalizing click callback in Chrome over an accumulated session (median and worst).

Output: capture-app view switching and pass-list navigation, paint.js glyph placement, index.html styling, test/paint.test.cjs, twelve-line check-paint harness, suffix-tolerant capture round-trip check, callback timings and the Chrome live-callback budget check.
</objective>

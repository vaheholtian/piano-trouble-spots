## Phase Goal

**As a** pianist drilling right-hand C D E F G alone at the FP-60X, **I want to** see wrong, missed and extra notes painted on the notation after each pass, with honest counts across the passes at one tempo, **so that** I can tell a recurring mistake from a one-off slip and trust what the score shows me.

<objective>
Close the phase the way the project constraint demands: re-run every automated gate fresh, give the user a plain checklist to keep open at the piano with the exact sentences to expect, and then stop for the real checks at the Roland FP-60X in the roadmap's order — rung 1 single passes, the rung-1 10+ pass habit-versus-slip experiment, and only then rungs 2 to 5. A false mark at any rung goes back to gap closure before the next rung.

Purpose: ROADMAP Phase 3 success criteria 4 and 5 are the only proof that the marks say what actually happened on the real hardware, and the ladder order is how the user builds trust one rung at a time.

Output: README.md and fixtures/README.md updated, all gates green, and the user's checkpoint responses recorded verbatim in the SUMMARY.
</objective>

Scenario A - single passes (read the score after each mark; use the pass list to look at one pass alone when told to):
1. Play C D E F G cleanly on the clicks, mark. Expect: no coloured noteheads, `Pass 1 - 5 notes`, heading `This session at 80 BPM - 1 passes`; click C4 -> `C4, bar 1 beat 1: played correctly in all 1 assessed passes`.

4. Correction: C D F E F G (a wrong F, then the right E, then F G), mark. Expect: a red `+1` above the staff between D4 and E4; click it -> `Extra notes between D4 and E4: in 1 of 4 assessed passes (F4 once)`; click E4 -> wrong in 1 of 4 (the E you corrected counts as played).
5. Abandon: play C D E, stop playing, mark before F's click. Expect: click pass 5 in the list -> F4 and G4 grey, C D E black; click F4 -> `F4, bar 1 beat 4: unassessed (not reached)`; click the heading -> F4 detail ends with `1 passes unassessed (1 not reached)`.
6. Restart without a mark: C D E, pause a full bar, then C D E F G from the next accented click, mark. Expect: the list shows `Pass 6 - 8 notes (restarted)`; in that pass view every notehead is grey; click any -> `... unassessed (restarted)`.
7. Double mark: press B7+C8 twice in a row. Expect: `Pass 7 - 0 notes (empty)`; no count changes anywhere.
8. The pair to confirm: (a) play the whole bar starting one beat late (C on beat 2's click, and so on), mark -> in that pass view no coloured noteheads (the app reads it as all played, a beat late; timing comes in Phase 4); (b) play D E F G A on the five clicks, mark -> five red noteheads, click C4 -> `C4, bar 1 beat 1: wrong, played D4`. 9. Drag the window to half width and back: the colours and the `+` stay on the right noteheads.

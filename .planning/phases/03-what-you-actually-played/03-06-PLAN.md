**As a** pianist drilling right-hand C D E F G alone at the FP-60X, **I want to** see wrong, missed and extra notes painted on the notation after each pass, with honest counts across the passes at one tempo, **so that** I can tell a recurring mistake from a one-off slip and trust what the score shows me.

<objective>

Each rule gets hand-derived fixtures first, then the code, then the tunables table if anything moved.

Purpose: ROADMAP success criterion 2's "without chords ... producing false mistakes" and criterion 1 on every rung, so the rungs 2-5 piano session (03-07) starts from green, trustworthy fixtures.

Output: four fixtures, in-chord ambiguity in src/align.js, test/align-ladder.test.cjs.
</objective>

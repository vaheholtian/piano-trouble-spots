## Phase Goal

**As a** pianist drilling right-hand C D E F G alone at the FP-60X, **I want to** see wrong, missed and extra notes painted on the notation after each pass, with honest counts across the passes at one tempo, **so that** I can tell a recurring mistake from a one-off slip and trust what the score shows me.

<objective>
Close the phase on the harder rungs, one rung at a time, after rung 1 was approved (03-05) and the chord, both-hands and ladder engine work landed (03-06): re-run every automated gate fresh, give the user a rungs 2-5 checklist whose every observation names the view and the exact sentence, then stop for the real checks at the FP-60X. A false mark at a rung stops the ladder there and goes to gap closure.

This plan is a verification gate with no production code layer, so it leads with the automated gate rather than a tracer slice; the tracer for this phase is 03-02.

Purpose: ROADMAP Phase 3 success criterion 4 on every rung, and the rung-5 hardware confirmation of the late-entry reading and the glyph placement across a line break.

Output: fixtures/README.md updated, all gates green, and the user's checkpoint response recorded verbatim in the SUMMARY.
</objective>

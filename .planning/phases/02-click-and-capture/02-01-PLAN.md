## Phase Goal

**As a** pianist drilling a passage alone, **I want to** play it many times to a metronome and have every repetition recorded as raw MIDI that nothing can lose, **so that** later phases can tell me what I keep getting wrong from data I trust.

<objective>
Lead the phase with its thinnest real path: pick the piano from the MIDI inputs, press Start, play, press Stop, close the tab, reopen — and the piece comes back from stored bytes with every raw MIDI message still there, unmodified, in arrival order. Then pin the two contracts every later plan and phase depends on (the decode table and the IndexedDB schema) with node:test.

Purpose: raw events are the source of truth for Phases 3 to 5 and the export in Phase 7 (D-17, one-way). This plan makes them durable before anything else is built on them, and proves the double-click-from-disk run path still holds with Web MIDI and IndexedDB in play (D-18).

Output: src/midi-capture.js, src/storage.js, src/capture-app.js, a two-line hook in src/score-renderer.js, index.html transport and indicator DOM plus the idb tag, scripts/check-run-path.cjs extended, scripts/check-capture-roundtrip.cjs (headless Chrome, fake MIDI, reload, assert), test/idb-node-env.cjs, test/midi-capture.test.cjs, test/storage.test.cjs.

No checkpoint:decision is inserted before the storage task because the user already chose this exact option in discuss-phase (planner-reversibility "gating a decision already made"); the rating is carried on the task for the record, and the record field names are decided here under the "exact raw event record format" discretion D-17 grants.

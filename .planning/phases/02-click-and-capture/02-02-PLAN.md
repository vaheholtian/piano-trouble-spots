## Phase Goal

**As a** pianist drilling a passage alone, **I want to** play it many times to a metronome and have every repetition recorded as raw MIDI that nothing can lose, **so that** later phases can tell me what I keep getting wrong from data I trust.

<objective>
Make Start audible and trustworthy: a Web Audio look-ahead click at the user's BPM with beat 1 accented from the score's time signature, every click written to the session's timeline as the expected timeline Phase 4 will measure against, and a clock pair sampled at Start so MIDI timestamps and AudioContext time can be compared. Then add the diagnostic readout that settles the MIDI-clock versus audio-clock question at the piano (roadmap success criterion 2), store the resulting calibration on the session, remember BPM per piece, and allow a mid-session tempo change.

Purpose: one scheduler produces both the sound and the ground truth (ARCHITECTURE Key Data Flow 4). If the click drifts or the clocks disagree, every later timing judgement is wrong, so this plan measures the relationship instead of assuming it (STATE.md Phase 2 blocker).

Output: src/clock.js, src/metronome.js, BPM/Start/Stop/readout wiring in src/capture-app.js and index.html, the run-path gate extended, the headless round trip extended to assert the click timeline, test/clock.test.cjs, test/metronome.test.cjs.
</objective>

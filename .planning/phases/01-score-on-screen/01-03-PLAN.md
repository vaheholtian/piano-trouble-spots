## Phase Goal

**As a** pianist practising alone, **I want to** open a tiny MusicXML exercise and see it as real notation, **so that** every note is modelled precisely enough that alignment can be built on it.

<objective>
Close the phase the way the project constraint demands: run every automated gate once more (shell-neutral node scripts, including the headless-Chrome map check across all six ladder files), hand the user a plain manifest of what each ladder file should look like, and then stop for the real check at the Roland FP-60X — every rung opened in Chrome by double-click, right notes on the right staff, every notehead mapped to its own notehead with map OK, surviving a resize on every rung, plus the .xml and .mxl paths and the invalid-file recovery.

Purpose: the prototype was never played once. ROADMAP success criterion 4 ("User sat at the FP-60X with the laptop and loaded each ladder file") is the only proof that SCORE-01 is met in the environment that will actually be used. The headless check makes sure the piano session is spent on musical judgement, not on a software defect.

Output: fixtures/README.md, a green full suite and check scripts, and the user's approval (or a list of what was wrong) recorded in the SUMMARY.
</objective>
